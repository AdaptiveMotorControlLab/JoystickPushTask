# Joystick shaft

STEP model of the 89 mm joystick shaft that both interchangeable handles mount onto.

- STEP source: [`joystick_89mm.step`](joystick_89mm.step)
- STL export: [`joystick_89mm.stl`](joystick_89mm.stl)
- Approximate CAD extent: 89 mm along the shaft axis; conical body from a 4 mm-radius base
  toward a 1.5 mm-radius tip
- Both the [bar handle](../joystick-handle-bar/README.md) and the
  [rounded-cube handle](../joystick-handle-cube/README.md) attach at the tip
- Not yet printed, fitted, or validated on the rig
