# Joystick rounded-cube handle

Interchangeable rounded-box / cube handle that slides onto the tip of the
[89 mm joystick shaft](../joystick/README.md). This is the current push object:
a compact contact surface meant to sit below the mouse and encourage a forward
push rather than a grasp.

## Original (20 mm wide)

- STEP source: [`joystick_handle_cube.step`](joystick_handle_cube.step)
- STL export: [`joystick_handle_cube.stl`](joystick_handle_cube.stl)
- CAD extent: 20 (lateral) × 11.5 (push) × 10 (height) mm, 3 mm corner radius,
  2.5 mm top edge fillets
- Three blind 3.2 mm holes, 3.2 mm deep, along the push axis (push face 2.05 /
  5.75 / 9.45 mm from the shaft axis). The shaft goes in the front hole (nearest
  the push face, the mouse side) so the paw cannot touch the cube and the rest
  pad together. Printed holes come out ~0.3–0.4 mm small; they still pass the
  3 mm shaft
- Used on the rig from the first mouse session (16 Oct 2026); position: front
  face 6 mm from the platform edge, 7 mm from the rest-pad edge

## Narrow cube (11.5 mm wide)

- STEP: [`joystick_handle_cube_11p5.step`](joystick_handle_cube_11p5.step),
  STL: [`joystick_handle_cube_11p5.stl`](joystick_handle_cube_11p5.stl),
  generated by [`make_cube_11p5.py`](make_cube_11p5.py) (netgen-mesher + numpy)
- 11.5 × 11.5 × 10 mm: the same handle with the two rounded ends moved 4.25 mm
  inward each. Push face, depth, height, fillets and the three holes are
  unchanged, so the force models apply as they are; volume 1131 mm³ instead of
  2085 mm³ (lighter handle)
- Not yet printed or fitted
