# Joystick rounded-cube handle

Interchangeable rounded-box / cube handle that slides onto the tip of the
[89 mm joystick shaft](../joystick/README.md). This is the current push-object
candidate: a compact contact surface meant to sit below the mouse and encourage
a forward push rather than a grasp.

- STEP source: [`joystick_handle_cube.step`](joystick_handle_cube.step)
- STL export: [`joystick_handle_cube.stl`](joystick_handle_cube.stl)
- Approximate CAD extent: 20 × 11.5 × 10.1 mm rounded box
- Three mounting holes let the cube sit at different offsets along the shaft.
  That changes how close the front face is to the mouse and rest pad, and it
  also shifts the object's weight relative to the joystick beneath it
- Alternative to the [bar handle](../joystick-handle-bar/README.md)
- Not yet printed, fitted, or validated on the rig. Spring return, steel washer
  target, and rest-pad-to-object geometry still need to be frozen after a
  physical fit check
