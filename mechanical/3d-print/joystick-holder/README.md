# Joystick component holder

Printed holder that bolts to the breadboard and carries the
[joystick component](../joystick-component/README.md); the
[1D-axis constrainer](../joystick-1d-axis-constrainer/README.md) slides over its top square.

- STEP source: [`joystick_holder.step`](joystick_holder.step)
- STL export: [`joystick_holder.stl`](joystick_holder.stl)
- Base 86 × 31.8 × 5 mm with four 10 × 24 mm slots (two per side of the 86 mm length); four 5 × 5 mm
  corner posts (z 5-20.4) with open sides for the cable; top frame z 20.41-25.41, 31.82 mm square, with
  a 25.3 mm hole.
- The component's body drops through the hole and its 29.75 mm flange rests on the frame, so the body
  bottom is 13.88 mm and the shaft base 40.5 mm above the breadboard.
- The constrainer's lower 10 mm (inner 32 mm) slides over the frame and rests on its top, so the
  constrainer spans z 15.41-71.41.
- The push runs along the 31.8 mm width (across the base); the Ledex stands on the breadboard on the
  mouse (home) side.
