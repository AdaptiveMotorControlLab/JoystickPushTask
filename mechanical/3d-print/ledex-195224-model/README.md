# Ledex 195224-230 reference model

Body and plunger of the Ledex STA 20 × 40 mm pull solenoid (60° plunger, no anti-rotation flat),
from the datasheet drawing (page F34, `simulations/ledex_femm/datasheet/`). Reference geometry for the
clearance checks, figures and the rig explorer; not for printing. Generated by
[`../ledex-holder-adjustable/make_ledex_holder.py`](../ledex-holder-adjustable/make_ledex_holder.py)
from the dimensions in `simulations/ledex_femm/ledex_geometry.py` (`LEDEX`, `plunger_dims`), the same
source the FEMM and 3D magnetic models use.

Frame: X along the axis from the body face (the boss side, + toward the joystick), axis on X; the
plunger is drawn seated (energised). On the rig the plunger moves out by the stroke.

| plunger length | 59.7 (55.9 cylinder + 3.8 cone) | from the mass, steel 7.85 g/cm³ |
| 60° cone | half-angle 37.5°, 1 mm flat at the apex | FEMM calibration |
| leads | 2 × 0.20 mm², ≥ 250 mm, leave the rear end at the bottom | drawing (drawn pointing down) |
| mounting nut | M14 × 1.5, shipped loose, size not given | assumed 22 mm AF × 3.5 mm |

The meshes weigh 20.0 g (plunger) and 86 g (body + plunger + lead stubs, as steel) against the
datasheet's 20.1 g and 83.6 g. Internal parts (bobbin, coil, back stop) are not modelled here; the
magnetic models in `simulations/ledex_femm/` add fitted internals to this outer geometry. The 3D model
uses this plunger as drawn (slot and cross hole); FEMM, being axisymmetric, uses equal-area and
equal-volume stand-ins for them.

To confirm with calipers before printing the link: slot width, cross-hole diameter and its distance
from the tip, the seated protrusion (body face to tip), the plunger length and the nut's size.
