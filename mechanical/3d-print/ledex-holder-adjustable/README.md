# Adjustable Ledex holder, gauges and plunger link

Three-part holder for the Ledex `195224-230` that sets the solenoid's distance, lateral position and
height relative to the joystick, plus setting gauges, the plunger-link parts and a fit test. It replaces
the fixed [30 mm axis holder](../ledex-holder/README.md). All parts are generated by
[`make_ledex_holder.py`](make_ledex_holder.py) (netgen/OCC, dimensions from
`simulations/ledex_femm/rig_geometry.py`); rerun it after changing a dimension, and it re-checks the
clearances against the joystick holder, the constrainer and the joystick component, and those of the
moving link parts over the whole push (`python make_ledex_holder.py check` reruns only the checks).

The Ledex itself is modelled from its datasheet drawing in [`../ledex-195224-model/`](../ledex-195224-model/README.md)
(reference only, not for printing); the checks and the rig explorer use it.

## Print fit

Holes and slots carry FDM allowances (the `FIT` block at the top of `make_ledex_holder.py`). The cube's
3.4 mm holes only just pass the 3.0 mm shaft tip, so small holes print ~0.3-0.4 mm undersize here:
collar bore 3.7, pin holes 2.9 (2.5 mm pins), bar 1.8 mm (plunger slot 2.5 ± 0.3), fork gap 2.6, M14
boss hole 14.8, M3 holes 3.6, M4 holes 4.5, M3 / M4 nut pockets 5.9 / 7.5 across flats, M2 pilot 1.8.
**Print `fit_test` first** with the same printer and settings: find the shaft hole that slides freely
down the shaft, the pin hole that turns freely on a pin, and the bar tab that slides into the plunger's
slot and into the fork slots; if they differ from the values above, change `FIT` and rerun.

## Setting the two layouts

The X positions below are the Ledex body face (the boss side) relative to the joystick axis at neutral,
and Z is the Ledex axis above the breadboard.

- **Plunger link (recommended):** link pin 17.5 mm above the shaft base, so the Ledex axis is at Z 60.7
  (scale tick 60 plus 0.7). Hold the handle at neutral, push the plunger in until the 3 mm stroke gauge
  is flush with the tip, and lock the base slots. That puts the body face at X −42.05.
  The link uses the plunger's own cross hole (2.5/2.6 mm, 4.76 mm from the tip, through both cheeks of
  the tip slot); nothing is drilled. Turn the plunger so the slot is vertical and the hole runs along Y
  (the plunger is round and can turn in its bore, so the leads can point any way). Pins: 2.5 mm brass or
  A2 stainless dowels, or M2.5 A2 screws with lock nuts, 10 mm long (the plunger pin must stay inside the
  16 mm wide constrainer window); not plain steel next to the plunger tip.
- **Washer target:** tower centre on the axis, so Z 58.7. Gap 1 mm at neutral (1 mm gap gauge between
  the tip and the washers), which puts the face at X −29.3. The nut front is then 1.8 mm from the
  constrainer wall.

Clearances from the last run, with the constrainer's window revision: link layout ≥ 16 mm for the
holder parts (nut front X −33.5 against the constrainer wall at −19); washer layout carriage 5.3,
upright 4.3 and base 5.4 mm. Moving link parts from the home stop to a 15 mm push: link end to shaft
2.1 mm, pin ends to the constrainer 3.0 mm, collar to the constrainer 3.1 mm, plunger to the
constrainer 3.8 mm, plunger tip to collar > 5 mm; the bar has 0.35 mm play each side in the plunger's
slot and 0.4 mm in the fork.

## Assembly and printing

- Hardware: two M6 cap screws (breadboard), two M4 screws plus hex nuts (upright to base), four M3
  screws with washers plus four M3 hex nuts (carriage to upright), the Ledex M14 nut, and for the link
  one M2 set screw (M2 × 6 grub, or a short M2 screw) and two 2.5 mm pins (or M2.5 A2 screws with lock
  nuts), 10 mm long.
- Carriage height: drop the four M3 nuts into the pockets on the carriage's inner face (beside the
  case, above and below it), put the screws through the upright's slots from the outside and tighten
  them from outside with a hex key; the nuts stay reachable from the Ledex side as well.
- Collar: slide it down the shaft before the cube goes on, set it with the fork's pin 17.5 mm above the
  shaft base and the fork facing the Ledex, then tighten the M2 set screw against the shaft.
- The link needs the constrainer revision with the square window
  ([`../joystick-1d-axis-constrainer/`](../joystick-1d-axis-constrainer/README.md)); with the original
  arch the plunger passes only 1 mm under its top.
- Print the base and the upright flat on their bottom faces, the carriage on its outer flange face (the
  nut pockets face up; the cradle's curved face needs light support), the gauges and the fit test flat,
  the collar on its end face, and the link bar flat. PETG or PLA, ≥ 40 % infill; check that the boss
  plate is flat so the Ledex sits square.
- Order of setting: lateral (Y) so the plunger lines up with the shaft, then height (Z) on the scale,
  then distance (X) with a gauge. Re-check that the handle returns to home and that nothing touches the
  constrainer over the full push before powering the Ledex.
- Not printed or fit-checked yet.
