# Adjustable Ledex holder, gauges and plunger link

Three-part holder for the Ledex `195224-230` that sets the solenoid's distance, lateral position and
height relative to the joystick, plus setting gauges and the plunger-link parts. It replaces the fixed
[30 mm axis holder](../ledex-holder/README.md). All parts are generated by
[`make_ledex_holder.py`](make_ledex_holder.py) (netgen/OCC, dimensions from
`simulations/ledex_femm/rig_geometry.py`); rerun it after changing a dimension, and it re-checks the
clearances against the joystick holder, the constrainer and the joystick component, and those of the
moving link parts over the whole push (`python make_ledex_holder.py check` reruns only the checks).

| Part | Files | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base | `ledex_holder_base.step/.stl` | 48 × 48 × 10 mm. Two 7 mm slots along the push axis (Y ±12.6 mm) for M6 cap screws into the breadboard, heads in 11.5 × 6.5 mm channels; 33 mm of distance travel; fits 25 mm and 1 inch hole grids. Two M4 hex-nut pockets from below. |
| Upright | `ledex_holder_upright.step/.stl` | 6 mm foot with two lateral M4 slots (±12.75 mm) onto the base nuts, a 5 mm wall with two vertical M3 slots, two ribs, and a height scale (tick every 1 mm, longer at 5 and 10 mm) for Ledex axis heights 30-70 mm above the breadboard. |
| Carriage | `ledex_holder_carriage.step/.stl` | 5 mm boss plate (14.5 mm hole for the M14 boss, nut on the joystick side), side flange with two M3 holes 20 mm apart, a cradle under the case, and an index notch on the flange's rear edge at the axis height. |
| Gap gauges | `gap_gauge_{0.5,1,2,3}mm` | Shims (40 × 15 mm, notch count = size order) to set the plunger-to-washer gap. |
| Stroke gauges | `stroke_gauge_{2,3,4}mm` | Forks that sit on the boss end around the plunger; the far end is flush with the plunger tip at that stroke (fork length 14.29 mm + stroke). |
| Link collar | `link_collar.step/.stl` | Split collar for the 3 mm shaft (6 mm tall), clevis on the home side with a 2.6 mm pin hole 5 mm from the shaft axis and a 2.3 mm slot for the bar; M2 clamp ear on the side (+Y), because on the far side it would hit the constrainer near a 15 mm push. |
| Link bar | `link_bar.step/.stl` | 2.0 × 5 mm bar, 2.6 mm holes 10 mm apart, rounded ends (R 2.5); goes into the plunger's 2.5 mm tip slot. |

The Ledex itself is modelled from its datasheet drawing in [`../ledex-195224-model/`](../ledex-195224-model/README.md)
(reference only, not for printing); the checks and the rig explorer use it.

## Setting the two layouts

The X positions below are the Ledex body face (the boss side) relative to the joystick axis at neutral,
and Z is the Ledex axis above the breadboard.

- **Plunger link (recommended):** link pin 18 mm above the shaft base, so the Ledex axis is at Z 58.5
  (scale tick 58 plus half a tick). Hold the handle at neutral, push the plunger in until the 3 mm
  stroke gauge is flush with the tip, and lock the base slots. That puts the body face at X −37.05.
  The link uses the plunger's own cross hole (2.5/2.6 mm, 4.76 mm from the tip, through both cheeks of
  the tip slot); nothing is drilled. Turn the plunger so the slot is vertical and the hole runs along Y
  (the plunger is round and can turn in its bore, so the leads can point any way). Pin: 2.5 mm brass or
  A2 stainless dowel, or an M2.5 A2 screw with a lock nut, 10 mm long (it must stay inside the 16 mm
  wide constrainer window); not plain steel next to the plunger tip.
- **Washer target:** tower centre on the axis, so Z 56.0. Gap 1 mm at neutral (1 mm gap gauge between
  the tip and the washers), which puts the face at X −29.3. The nut front is then 1.8 mm from the
  constrainer wall.

Clearances from the last run, with the constrainer's window revision: link layout ≥ 11.4 mm for the
holder parts (nut front X −28.5 against the constrainer wall at −19); washer layout carriage 5.3,
upright 4.3 and base 5.4 mm. Moving link parts from the home stop to a 15 mm push: link end to shaft
1.0 mm (inside the clevis), plunger tip to collar 1.4 mm, pin ends to the constrainer 3.0 mm, collar to
the constrainer 3.3 mm, plunger to the constrainer 4.1 mm.

## Assembly and printing

- Hardware: two M6 cap screws (breadboard), two M4 screws plus hex nuts (upright to base), two M3 screws
  plus nuts or washers (carriage to upright), the Ledex M14 nut, and for the link one M2 clamp screw and
  two 2.5 mm pins (or M2.5 A2 screws with lock nuts), 10 mm long.
- The link needs the constrainer revision with the square window
  ([`../joystick-1d-axis-constrainer/`](../joystick-1d-axis-constrainer/README.md)); with the original
  arch the plunger passes only 1 mm under its top.
- Print the base and the upright flat on their bottom faces, the carriage on its outer flange face (the
  cradle's curved face needs light support), the gauges flat, the collar on its end face, and the link bar
  flat. PETG or PLA, ≥ 40 % infill; check that the boss plate is flat so the Ledex sits square.
- Order of setting: lateral (Y) so the plunger lines up with the shaft, then height (Z) on the scale,
  then distance (X) with a gauge. Re-check that the handle returns to home and that nothing touches the
  constrainer over the full push before powering the Ledex.
- Not printed or fit-checked yet.
