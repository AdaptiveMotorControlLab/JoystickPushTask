# Push object — superseded laser-cut contact plate

> **Superseded 04 Sep 2026.** Rig positioning showed that a plate placed in front of the mouse's
> nose would be difficult to reach. The active design is now the 3D-modeled
> [joystick shaft](../../3d-print/joystick/README.md) plus interchangeable tip-mounted handles
> ([rounded cube](../../3d-print/joystick-handle-cube/README.md) or
> [bar](../../3d-print/joystick-handle-bar/README.md)), meant to sit below the mouse and
> encourage a forward push rather than the grasp used for the original pull handles.

This 03 Sep 2026 prototype is retained as design history: 36 × 36 mm, 3 mm transparent sheet,
rounded top edge, and square base edge for glue-up to the joystick handle. Do not fabricate it as
the current push-object design.

- Cut-ready source: [`laser_cut_36mm_square_left_rounded.svg`](laser_cut_36mm_square_left_rounded.svg)
- Preview: [`push-object-contact-plate_36mm_preview.png`](push-object-contact-plate_36mm_preview.png)

The material appears to be clear acrylic/plexiglass but has not been positively identified.

## Ferromagnetic target

The axial Ledex solenoid attracts a steel target on the replacement 3D-modeled handle; it is not
mechanically linked to the solenoid plunger. The target requirements remain applicable even though
this contact plate is superseded. Two galvanized carbon-steel washer options were ordered from SFS
Switzerland on 04 Sep 2026:

- M4 article `111952`: DIN 125-1-A / ~ISO 7089, steel 140 HV, zinc-plated blue,
  4.3 mm ID × 9 mm OD × 0.8 mm
- M3 article `132845`: DIN 9021 / ~ISO 7093-1, steel 140 HV, zinc-plated blue,
  3.2 mm ID × 9 mm OD × 0.8 mm

Stack five matching washers for a nominal 4.0 mm target and test both bore options. Do not
substitute A2/A4 stainless steel, aluminium, brass, plastic, or permanent magnets. The final target
still requires attachment to the handle and force calibration with the solenoid mounted on the
home side so attraction opposes the push.
